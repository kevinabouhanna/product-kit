# Phase 1: Out of NetworkMonitor

**Milestone:** Phase 1: Out of NetworkMonitor · **Status:** Done

**Goal:** the three tools NetworkMonitor grew to keep its docs, pictures and languages current,
out of NetworkMonitor and into one plugin any project can install, proved on a second Mac app.
The plan was #1 (closed 2026-11-13, when phase 2 took over what it had left).

## What shipped

- **docs-kit moved here** with its history, and the repository set up for parallel sessions (#2).
- **lang-kit**: every language held to its glossary, pages with their copies, the reviewer's
  list, adding a language (#4).
- **shots-kit**: the website's pictures kept current, for any macOS app (#6); a project with no
  website whose app draws its own pictures, claude-profiles-mac (#8); pictures taken in a
  throwaway VM (#10), set up like the Mac (#12).
- **The plan, docs-kit's first stage**: phases as milestones, issues, the board and pages (#18),
  the plan as a tree of issues (#22), the Inbox with a Kind (#26, #28), and fixes (#20, #24).
- Upkeep: two pull requests at once in local CI (#14), the worktree hooks left to shared-mac
  (#16).

NetworkMonitor, claude-profiles-mac and home-control run on these tools.
